Puli · Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly.

We don’t collect any data from Puli.

What data do we collect?

{%- comment -%} What a visitor might not know we see first, then the mailing list, then the app (Alan, Nov 19). {%- endcomment %}

Our web host, like any, sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page, but we don’t collect or keep it.

If you join our mailing list, we will collect your email address. We use it only to send you news about Puli. The list runs on Kit, an email service provider, which stores your address and sends our emails.

If you email support, we have your address. We use it only to respond to you. Emailing support will not add your address to our mailing list.

We never sell, rent, or share email addresses (beyond Kit).

Puli itself does not collect any information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads collecting metadata.

Where are your notes?

On your Mac, encrypted. If you back them up to iCloud, they’re encrypted end to end, with keys only your Macs have. We never see them. Neither does anyone else. Not even Apple.

Who do we share data with?

Our email service provider, Kit, if you sign up for our mailing list.

We do not share data with anyone else.

Puli never talks to us. It talks to Apple directly only when you ask it:

  • To back up to iCloud.
  • To buy lessons. Apple handles the payment, and tells Puli which lessons you own.
  • To send crash reports, if you share analytics with developers in your Mac’s settings.

Apple never shares your personal information with us.

How long do we keep it?

We keep your emails on our mailing list until you unsubscribe, or ask us to remove them.

What are your rights?

Ask what we have, and we’ll tell you. Ask us to delete it, and we will.

Changes

This policy may change. If it does, we’ll update this page and the date below.
