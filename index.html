01Why Puli

Thoughts are fleeting. Write them down.

Everyone’s racing to record, transcribe, and summarize your meetings. But transcripts only capture what was said. A note captures what you thought. Puli tends your thoughts.

Preferably somewhere with good recall.

Other note-taking apps make you pick a folder. But a note about Dana and the Q3 launch belongs to both. Tag them, and Puli puts the note in both places, ready to recall the next time either is mentioned.

02How Puli Works

Just start writing. Puli takes it from there.

People

Everyone you talk to has a page.

Type @Ben-Adler in any note, and that line lands on Ben’s page, with everything else you’ve written about them. Before your next 1:1, it’s all there.

03Pricing

Free. Forever.

Taking notes and capturing tasks are always free. You can teach Puli new tricks by purchasing lessons.

Free

  • Notes in Markdown
  • People and projects, filed as you write
  • Their pages beside your note, when you need them
  • Every task from every note in one place
  • Multiple journals, with templates
  • Import from Notes and Markdown
  • Export to Markdown
  • VoiceOver, Voice Control, and the keyboard for everything

Lessons

  • Today Your day, with meetings and what’s due
  • Triage Task priorities, due dates, sorting, and grouping
  • Progress A board for tracking projects
  • Explore How your people and projects connect
  • Suggestions Tags and tasks, suggested as you write
  • Themes Handpicked colors for every journal
  • Typography Curated fonts and line spacing for every journal

04Promises

Three promises.

We will never abuse your privacy.

No recordings, transcripts, or bots. Your notes live on your Macs and in your own iCloud, encrypted with keys in your keychain. We never see them. Apple can’t even see them.

We will never steal your attention.

The app is intentionally calm. There are no red bubbles and no advertisements to break your train of thought.

We will never revoke your purchases.

Once you purchase a lesson, it’s yours to keep. You paid for it. It’s yours.

05Questions

Questions?

Still curious? Ask us.

Why is it called Puli?

It’s named for a Hungarian herding dog, famous for its corded coat. Say it POO-lee.

A herding dog doesn’t replace the shepherd. The shepherd decides where the sheep go, and the dog keeps the flock together. You decide what matters. Puli makes sure none of it wanders off.

Is Puli really free?

Yes. Everything you need to capture and herd your notes is free, forever. You can improve your experience by purchasing lessons to teach Puli new tricks.

Do I keep the lessons I buy?

Yes. Every lesson you purchase is yours to keep. You paid for it. It’s yours.

Does Puli record my meetings?

No. Absolutely not. No recordings. No transcripts. And no bots sending your notes to far-off data centers.

Where are my notes kept?

On your Mac, and if you choose, in your own iCloud, encrypted end to end. We never see your notes. And neither does anyone else. Not even Apple.

What do I need?

Puli works on any Mac running macOS 15 or later.

Some features can leverage your Mac’s onboard Apple Intelligence, which requires a Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.

06About

After a decade of managing people and many years of recorded meetings, I realized I didn’t need to capture more information. And I didn’t need transcripts of everything that was said. What I wanted was a place to capture my thoughts and surface them again when relevant.

I built Puli to herd your thoughts into all the right places, so the context you need is right there when you need it.

Alan Ridlehoover
Founder, Deliberate Software
