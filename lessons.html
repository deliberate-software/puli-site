Puli

How lessons work.

Puli is free, forever. Lessons train Puli to perform new tricks.

Instincts

Puli is an instinctive herder. It comes with everything you need to keep your notes organized, from Markdown support, to tagging people, groups, and projects, to writing and checking off tasks, to fetching relevant context on command. Those features are free, forever.

Lessons

Lessons are how Puli learns new tricks. When you retain our trainers, Puli immediately learns every trick in our book. Plus, Puli never forgets a lesson you paid for, even if you stop paying for future lessons.

We offer two plans:

  • Our yearly plan includes every lesson in our book, plus 12 months of new lessons as they are released. This is a recurring plan that offers a 30-day free trial. You can cancel anytime.
  • Our forever home plan provides Puli with a lifetime of learning. Puli will learn every lesson we ever release, for a one-time, fixed price.

The lessons

There are seven lessons available to Puli as of our launch. They are:

  • Today: walk into every meeting prepared.
  • Triage: know what to do next.
  • Progress: track where every project stands.
  • Explore: see the bigger picture.
  • Themes: make each journal feel like home.
  • Typography: make every word a pleasure to read.
  • Suggestions: let Puli do the tagging.

Job Search journals include Today, Triage, Progress, and Explore for free.

Getting lessons

Choose Help › Take Puli for a Walk… to try a fully trained Puli on a sample journal. You won’t be able to save the journal. But you will be able to see all of Puli’s lessons in action.

Choose Help › Sign Up for Lessons… to sign up for lessons. Or, if you’ve previously purchased lessons, click Restore Purchases.

Thank you!

Thank you for adopting Puli. Our professional trainers are standing by to lovingly teach your Puli new tricks.
