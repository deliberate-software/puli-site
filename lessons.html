Puli

How lessons work.

Puli is free, forever. Lessons train Puli to perform new tricks.

Instincts

Puli is an instinctive herder. It comes with everything you need to keep your notes organized, from Markdown support, to tagging people, groups, and projects, to writing and checking off tasks, to fetching relevant context on command. Those features are free, forever.

Lessons

Lessons are how Puli learns new tricks. When you retain our trainers, Puli immediately learns every trick in our book. Plus, Puli never forgets a lesson you paid for, even if you stop paying for future lessons.

We offer two plans:

  • Our yearly plan includes every lesson in our book, plus 12 months of new lessons as they are released. This is a recurring plan that offers a one-month free trial. You can cancel anytime.
  • Our forever home plan provides Puli with a lifetime of learning. Puli will learn every lesson we ever release, for a one-time, fixed price.

The lessons

There are seven lessons available to Puli as of our launch. Job Search journals include Today, Triage, Progress, and Explore for free.

Today

Walk into every meeting prepared. See your day beside your notes: your meetings, what’s due, and what you’ve written. Take notes for a meeting with one click.

Triage

Know who’s on the hook for what. Filter and group your tasks, see each page’s open tasks, and see what’s due wherever you are.

Progress

Track where every project stands. Give your projects stages, like To do, Doing, and Done, and track their progress on a board.

Explore

See the bigger picture. See how the people, groups, and projects in your notes connect, through the notes they share.

Themes

Make each journal feel like home. Give each journal its own colors, from five light themes and five dark.

Make every word a pleasure to read. Give each journal its own fonts, line spacing, and column width.

Suggestions

Let Puli do the tagging. Puli spots the tags and tasks you don’t, so you can accept or decline them.

How to use Suggestions

Getting lessons

Choose Help › Take Puli for a Walk… to try a fully trained Puli on a sample journal. You won’t be able to save the journal. But you will be able to see all of Puli’s lessons in action.

Choose Help › Sign Up for Lessons… to sign up for lessons. Or, if you’ve previously purchased lessons, click Restore Purchases.
