Puli · Handler’s Guide

Themes.

Give each journal its own colors, light or dark.

Light or dark

Each journal is light or dark by its theme, whatever your Mac is set to. So one journal can be light while another, open beside it, is dark. Its menus, scroll bars, and sheets match it.

A new journal starts in Paper if your Mac is light, or Slate if it’s dark. Puli’s own windows, like Settings and About, follow your Mac.

Choose a theme

Open the journal, then Settings › Themes. Click a theme, and the journal changes at once.

  • Light: Paper, Cream, Frost, Sage, and Blush.
  • Dark: Slate, Midnight, Graphite, Ink, and Plum.

Each journal keeps its own theme, on all your devices. Tags keep their colors in every theme. A dark journal prints in light colors.

A lesson

Paper and Slate are always yours. Themes is a lesson that adds the other eight. It’s free in the sample journals. See How Lessons Work.

If your lessons end before you’ve learned Themes, a journal in a light theme goes back to Paper, and one in a dark theme to Slate.
