Puli · Handler’s Guide

Explore.

See how the people, groups, and projects in your notes connect, through the notes they share.

A lesson

Explore is a lesson that reveals how your notes connect on the Graph view. It’s free in the Job Search and sample journals. See How Lessons Work.

{%- comment -%} The lesson is Explore, for what it lets you do; the view it adds is still Graph, with ⌃⌘G (Alan, Nov 21) {%- endcomment %}

Open the Graph

Choose View › Graph, press ⌃⌘G, or click Graph in the sidebar. Each person, group, and project in your notes is a tag, drawn with its kind’s icon from the sidebar, such as a person, a building, or a folder. The more notes a tag is in, the bigger it is. The more notes two tags share, the closer they sit.

Center a tag

Click a tag to center it. Its page opens in the context pane, and lines join it to the people, groups, and projects it shares the most notes with. Choose how many from the Top dropdown. Press Esc, or click the empty space, to let go.

Move around

Pinch to zoom, or press + and −. Drag the empty space to move everything, or drag a tag to move it.

Choose what to see

Use the dropdowns at the top right. Show picks the kinds, such as only people. Tasks shows only those with open tasks. Active shows only those mentioned lately, such as in the last 7 days.
