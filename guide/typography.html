Puli · Handler’s Guide

Typography.

Give each journal its own fonts, line spacing, and column width.

Choose the fonts

Open the journal, then Settings › Typography. Click a pairing of fonts for headings and text, and the journal changes at once.

A new journal starts in Editorial. Editorial and System are always yours.

Each journal keeps its own fonts, on all your devices. Puli’s own windows, like Settings and About, keep Puli’s fonts.

Line spacing and the Focus column

With the lesson, choose beneath the fonts how far apart your lines are, Tight, Normal, or Roomy, and how wide your note is in Focus: Narrow, Normal, or Wide.

A lesson

Typography is a lesson that adds eight more pairings: Classic, Book, Document, Writer, Modern, Round, Airy, and Marker. It also adds Line spacing and the Focus column. It’s free in the sample journals. See How Lessons Work.

If your lessons end before you’ve learned Typography, a journal goes back to Editorial, Normal line spacing, and the Normal Focus column. Puli remembers your choices, should you take lessons again.
