Puli · Handler’s Guide

Progress.

Give your projects stages, like To do, Doing, and Done, and track their progress on a board.

A lesson

Progress is a lesson that tracks where your projects stand in the Projects view. It’s free in the Job Search and sample journals. See How Lessons Work.

Add stages

Open Settings › Tags, and click Add Stages beneath Projects. You start with To do, Doing, and Done. Rename them, add more, or put them in another order.

A Job Search journal comes with stages for its roles, from Networking to Closed.

Tracking progress

Once the journal has stages, the Projects view appears. Choose View › Projects, press ⇧⌘P, or click the Projects view in the sidebar. Each stage is a column, and each project a card in it. Drag a card to another column to change its stage. Click a card to see its page in the context pane.

Choose what to see

Use the dropdowns at the top right. Tasks shows only projects with open tasks. Active shows only those mentioned lately. With shows only those mentioned with someone, such as Diane.

Priority from a stage

With the Triage lesson, a stage can have a priority, set beside its name in Settings › Tags. A task about a project in that stage takes its priority, unless the task gives its own.
