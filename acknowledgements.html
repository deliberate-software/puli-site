Acknowledgements

Puli stands on the shoulders of remarkably generous people. Your notes are stored by SQLite, encrypted by SQLCipher, and handled by GRDB, and every word is set in the fonts listed below. Their makers poured their skill into this work and then gave it away. Puli wouldn’t exist without them, and we’re deeply grateful.
