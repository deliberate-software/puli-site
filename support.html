How can we help?

Write to us at {{ site.email }}. We read every email, and we answer it ourselves.

What to include

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Puli’s version, from About Puli in the Puli menu.
  • Your Mac’s model and macOS version, from About This Mac in the Apple menu.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Leave out anything you’d rather we didn’t see.

Lessons

How Lessons Work explains each plan, and what you keep.

Lessons missing on a new Mac?

Choose Sign Up for Lessons… from the Help menu, then Restore Purchases.

Cancel the yearly plan

Cancel in your App Store subscriptions. You keep every lesson you have, and any that arrive before your year ends.

Cancel during the free trial

You won’t be charged. The lessons lock again, but your notes stay as they are, and the sample journals still have every lesson.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds, at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a purchase, its lessons lock again. Your notes are never touched.
