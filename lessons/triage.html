Triage.

Give tasks due dates and priorities, sort, filter, and group them, and see what’s due wherever you are.

{%- comment -%} Rewritten Nov 20: only the Tasks view is free, usable but not convenient; Triage is due dates, priorities, sorting, filtering, grouping, and a page's open tasks (Alan) {%- endcomment %}

A lesson

Triage is a lesson. It’s free in Job Search and sample journals. Without it, you can still write, start, and complete tasks, and see them all in the Tasks view; see Tasks and promises. See How Lessons Work.

Give a task a due date

Write when it’s due, in words: [] Send the deck by Fri. When you save, Puli writes the date in its place, as in by 2026-11-20. Press ⌘Z right after saving to get your words back.

by or due works with a day, a date, or a word, such as by Friday, due Nov 20, or by next week. today, tomorrow, and EOW work on their own.

Give a task a priority

Write P0, P1, P2, or P3 anywhere in the task. P0 is the most urgent: [] P0 Fix the outage.

In your notes, a due date shows as a pill, such as “Fri, Nov 20”, and a priority as a small label. A task past due shows in red.

Sort and reorder

In the Tasks view, click Task, Priority, or Due at the top to sort by it. To put tasks in your own order, drag one by the grab bar at the end of its row, or press ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓.

Change a priority or a due date

In the Tasks view, click a task’s priority or due date, or its “—”, and choose another. Puli changes it in the note.

Filter and group

Use the dropdowns beside the Tasks heading:

  • Show: Open, In progress, or Done tasks.
  • Due: Past due, Today, This week, and more, each with how many.
  • Group by: Priority; Owner, to see what each person promised you; or a kind of tag, such as People or Projects.

The sidebar also shows In progress, Past due, and Due today beneath Tasks. Click one to see those tasks.

Open tasks on a page

A person’s, group’s, or project’s page in the context pane shows its open tasks, above its notes. Click ↗ beside “Open tasks” to see them in the Tasks view.

What’s due, wherever you are

  • Click the dog in the menu bar to see what’s due today.
  • Puli’s widget shows What’s next: the tasks due and past due.
  • Ask Siri: “What’s due today in Puli?”
